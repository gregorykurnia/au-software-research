- **Solved well by free or bundled?** Partly, and well enough. A one-time export is free on both platforms (Xero reports, MYOB's 30-day AccountRight backup). Cheap bundled read-only options exist: MYOB A$2.50 a month, Xero Ledger A$7 a month through a partner. Xero reactivation stays possible for up to 7 years. Attachments are the only real hole on Xero, and five or more cheap apps fill it. MYOB now has MyDataXport for the same job.
- **Group served badly:** MYOB Business (browser) owners leaving or restructuring who can't back up a company file and won't pay forever: MI-62, MI-63, MI-64. But MI-64 explicitly refuses to pay a third party, and MyDataXport now targets exactly this group.
- **Recency:** The pain is long-running, since the 5-year rule doesn't change. But MYOB's A$2.50 offer ends 2026-12-31 (it may be renewed or dropped), and MYOB's AccountRight offline-file changes in 2026 may remove the free route. That is unsettled either way.
- **Compared to what?** The free one-time export, keeping the platform at A$2.50–7 a month, or reactivating Xero for a month when the ATO asks. Ten-year cost of a bundled option: about A$300–840.
- **Price anchor:** A$2.50 a month incl. GST (MYOB read-only); A$7 a month (Xero Ledger via a partner); US$8–25 a month (ControlC); one-off £10–300 (Numerint). **Hard limit 3:** fails for owners (a one-off event). A practice-facing version would need new evidence from accountants, and the ledger has none.

- **Solved well by free or bundled?** Partly. On MYOB, back-dating is solved by the built-in session-date audit report. On Xero, lock dates prevent most of it and XBert catches lock-date moves and missing attachments for practices. What's unsolved: a created-date view of entries for Xero users who aren't practices, compulsory job codes on MYOB, and compulsory tracking on Xero. Both platforms have declined the last two.
- **Group served badly:** MYOB office admins and bookkeepers fixing missing job codes after the fact (MI-42 B1, MI-41 B2). No MYOB built-in or third-party check was found. Xero users hunting back-dated postings without a practice tool (XI-015, B1, a single id).
- **Recency:** Durable. Xero has put compulsory tracking, an audit trail and a missing-receipt report "not in pipeline" or off the roadmap (2025). Only XI-027 is moving, and it is about access, not checks.
- **Compared to what?** Manual review at month end ("many hours", MI-42), or a practice paying XBert (A$7–99 per client per month) or Dext. On MYOB, running the free Journal Security Audit by session date.
- **Price anchor:** XBert A$7–99 per client per month plus A$29 per user; Dext Data Health add-on A$10 per client per month (3.1). Note the evidence is three different checks across two ledgers; only the MYOB job-code check is clearly unserved, and it rests on 2 people (MI-41, MI-42).

- **Solved well by free or bundled?** On Xero, mostly yes: docx themes cover hiding costs and lump sums, branding themes cover the bank account, and multiple addresses are in development. On MYOB Business, no: none of the asks are fixed or on the roadmap, and the bundled fix is upgrading to AccountRight at roughly double the price.
- **Group served badly:** MYOB Business (Lite/Pro) users whose customers dictate the invoice form: PDF attached (MI-31, MI-32, MI-33), job or store address (MI-36, MI-37), bank account per invoice (MI-66, MI-67), and hiding the cost summary (MI-17, MI-18). Xero users who want attachments sent by default (XI-028).
- **Recency:** Mixed. Xero's address gap will probably close within 2 years (in development). The MYOB Business gaps have lasted since 2024 with no roadmap item, but MYOB could add any one at any time (MI-21). Peppol will slowly remove the PDF-for-payables ask for buyers who register.
- **Compared to what?** Minutes per invoice by hand (emailing it to themselves, editing and forwarding, MI-18), a job-management app from about A$29 a month, or AccountRight (about A$95 a month more, list). No buyer states a money loss beyond "late payments" (MI-32).
- **Price anchor:** A Xero docx template from a freelancer, about US$35–150 one-off (S, 3.1); the MYOB Business Pro to AccountRight Plus gap of about A$95 a month list (S); job apps from about A$29 a month (S, unverified). A third party can't change MYOB's own invoice; it can only re-send its own PDF, which MYOB's Secure Invoicing change may make harder.
