- **Solved well by free or bundled?** Partly. Xero's built-in inventory covers basic finished-goods counts. Kits, PO receiving, landed cost and price levels aren't built in and aren't on Xero's AU roadmap with dates. Cheap apps cover them only at the very small end (Zoho free/A$43–54, Katana free up to 30 SKUs).
- **Group served badly:** two groups. (1) Small Xero product sellers who need one or two missing basics (kits, price levels, multi-currency prices) and are pushed into a whole second inventory system: XI-037, XI-042, XI-044, PR-X12, PR-X38. (2) Cin7 Core users hit by price rises who are looking to leave: XA-020, XA-022, XA-024, XA-025. The next tier down (Unleashed, inFlow, TidyStock) costs about A$130–600/month, so there is a price gap between free Zoho or Katana and US$186+.
- **Recency:** it still hurts in two years unless Xero ships bundling or brings XIP to AU. Neither has a date: bundling has been silent since June 2025, and XIP isn't promised for AU. Product categories (XI-043) are coming but don't touch the core asks. The Stocky shutdown is a one-off churn event for Shopify sellers, not a lasting pain.
- **Compared to what?** Today: Xero alone with workarounds (a duplicate item per price level, overwriting unit prices by hand), a spreadsheet, Zoho or Katana's free tiers, or Cin7 or Unleashed at about US$349–729/month. The real cost of leaving Cin7 is re-setup and lost history (XA-021: "migrating is a headache"), not the subscription.
- **Price anchor seen:** Zoho Standard about A$43–54/month incl. GST. TidyStock from NZ$127/month. inFlow from US$186/month. Cin7 Core from US$349/month (3.1).

- **Solved well by free or bundled?** Mostly yes for Xero: ABA export, per-supplier due dates, and now in-app bill payments (Airwallex, A$0.35) are built in, and credits inside a batch cost about A$25–35/month (Batchly). Not solved: holding a disputed bill (Xero says "longer term"), and supplier terms in MYOB Business.
- **Group served badly:** Xero bookkeepers who need a visible on-hold flag for disputed bills (XI-004, XI-005). No paid app found, only free user-built Chrome extensions. MYOB Business users who can't set supplier terms (MI-14, MI-15, MI-16), though AccountRight already has it, so they can move up a tier.
- **Recency:** the shrinking kind. In-app payments launched in 2026, Batchly launched in April 2026, and Xero has "Accepted" holds. In two years, likely only the hold flag (if Xero still hasn't shipped it) and MYOB Business terms remain, and both are single features, not a product.
- **Compared to what?** Today: Xero batch with native ABA plus applying credits one by one (XI-024), or Batchly/BatchPay at A$20–35/month. Holds via notes, a tracking category or far-off planned dates. MYOB Business users type due dates by hand (MI-14).
- **Price anchor seen:** BatchPay A$20/month. Batchly about A$25 or US$35/month (low). Xero bill payments A$0.35 per payment after the included allowance. ApprovalMax Premium about NZ$171 (3.1, low).
