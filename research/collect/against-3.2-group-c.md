**STP note:** a tool that only reads Xero/MYOB payroll data and flags problems, and never lodges STP or connects to ATO services, probably avoids product registration and whitelisting (those attach to ATO connections). The DSP definition's wording is broad, so this is **low-to-medium confidence: flag for the lawyer check**. It would still have to meet Xero's own app security standard.

- **Solved well by free or bundled?** Partly. The biggest gap (XI-010, 522 votes) is shipping in Xero "later this year"; award rates are free from Fair Work; award checking before pay runs is a crowded paid field. What's left are small UI gaps (MYOB inactive pay items, MYOB Team approvers, Xero U18 super alert) that only the platform can fix inside its own screens.
- **Group served badly:** none an outside tool could serve. The still-open gaps are MYOB users with stale pay items (MI-39, MI-40), MYOB Team employers moving staff between approvers (MI-51, MI-52) and Xero employers of under-18s (XI-012, one id). Each is a missing setting inside the platform, not a job a separate product owns. The only outside product shape, a read-only "pre-pay-run checker" (public holidays, U18 super, award minimums from FWC data), has no buyer id asking for a checker, and it competes with Deputy, Tanda and RosterElf, which prevent the errors at timesheet stage.
- **Recency:** Payroll pain is permanent, but the top item is being fixed now (working patterns, late 2026). In two years the residue is minor settings.
- **Compared to what?** Today: fix payslips by hand on public holidays; rename old pay items with "Z"; ask the accountant for setup (PR-X34, amount not stated); or pay A$8+/person for Deputy/Tanda-style award tools or A$5–20/employee/pay run for a bureau.
- **Price anchor seen:** Payroll A$0 (Payroller Lite) to A$3.99–5.50/employee/month; award tools A$8+/person (3.1); bureau A$5–20/employee/pay run (3.1).
