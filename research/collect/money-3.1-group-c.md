**Verdict:** plenty of B1 shows trades pay A$30–150+ per user per month for job management tools that include costing, and Simpro buyers report A$3k–6k setup fees. B1 for the exact gap (true job profit, progress claims) is thinner: Payapps (2021) and WorkGuru reviews name spreadsheet hours, plus the ledger's XI-033 and PR-X28. **Buyers pay today:** A$29–149/month (ServiceM8), about US$47–61 per user per month (Tradify), about A$70 per user per month (Fergus buyer), A$40–275/month for progress-claim tools. Against the candidate: the paid tools already sell costing and progress invoicing, so any gap sits inside them.
