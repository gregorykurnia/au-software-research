# 3.1 Money signals: group A (inventory-beside-xero, getting-paid, supplier-payment-runs)

Collected 2026-10-28. Xero App Store figures come from the AU listing's embedded data and its public reviews JSON (`apps.xero.com/api/reviews?appEntryId=…`). Newest 50 reviews scanned per app. **Caveats:** the store doesn't show reviewer country, so reviews are global, not proven Australian. Paidnice (83/83 five-star) and Chaser (365/374) look like solicited review sets. Debtor Daddy's money quotes are 2015–2020. Vendor prices are USD unless marked AUD, and ex-tax as shown.
