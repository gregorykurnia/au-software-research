# Xero App Store collection notes (2026-10-28)

Source: apps.xero.com/au/app/{slug}/reviews?rating=1..3 (curl, ~37 page loads). Cutoff 2023-10-27, 1-3 stars.
Ledger: research/collect/xero-appstore.csv (XA-001..XA-034).

Totals: 34 items. Segment B 18, D 11, A 5. B1 17, B2 17.
Per app: ServiceM8 7, Tradify 4, Fergus 3, Simpro 4, AroFlo 0, Cin7 Core 9, A2X 0, Starshipit 2, Dext 2, Hubdoc 3.

Shortfall vs target (~40): the listings are sorted newest first but most low-star reviews are old.
A2X and AroFlo had no 1-3 star reviews dated after 2023-10-27 on the pages read; every other app ran out of recent reviews.
Pages where all items were recent were followed to the next page (Cin7 rating 2); no more recent items found.

Dropped: non-AU context (Hubdoc VAT items, Simpro NZD review), vague rants, vendor replies.

Patterns:
- Cin7 Core (formerly Dear): repeated large yearly price rises, USD billing, support cuts (XA-019..XA-027); some say they will migrate.
- Xero connection/sync breaking: ServiceM8 (XA-001), Simpro (XA-016), Dext (XA-030), Hubdoc (XA-032).
- Trades apps: iOS-first (ServiceM8 Android limits, XA-004..XA-006), missing basic features (Fergus, Tradify), support not solving issues.
- Simpro: costly setup, price rises, renewal lock-in (XA-015, XA-017).

Problems: none with access. No instructions found in page content. Reviewer country not shown.
