# MYOB Idea Exchange (segment A): Chrome collection

Collected 2026-10-27 with Claude in Chrome on the owner's Mac. About 60 page loads, a few seconds apart.

- **Where it lives now:** `community.myob.com` (the `myob.lithium.com` links redirect there). There are two
  boards: MYOB Business Ideas Exchange (393 posts) and MYOB AccountRight Ideas Exchange (498 posts).
- **What was taken:** the MYOB Business top 30 by votes, with each idea page's first page of comments
  ("most liked", up to 10 comments). For AccountRight, only the top 30 list, without opening the idea pages,
  to keep the page count low.
- **Statuses on the new board:** OPEN, ON HOLD (MYOB parks ideas with low votes every 6 months), CLOSED
  (with a moderator note, this is MYOB's "declined") and DONE. "Not Likely" is no longer used.
- **Reposts:** on 2024-11-05..11, MYOB reposted old ideas with notes such as "originally posted 13 years ago
  with 118 votes". The post date is the repost date; the original age and votes are in the notes.
- **Excerpts:** verbatim, under 25 words, with names stripped. Every commenter is an MYOB user, and so a
  buyer. "Role" is only what the text states. Forum ranks such as "Experienced Cover User" are not roles;
  a "Partner" badge means an MYOB partner (an accountant or bookkeeper practice) and is noted as such.
- **Type:** B2 unless marked B1 (the buyer states money paid or lost, or hours spent). MYOB staff replies are S.
