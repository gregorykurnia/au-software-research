## Seller context (S, not evidence)

- **BankToSheet** (banktosheet.com.au): converts bank PDF statements for Xero/MYOB, hosted in Australia.
  Posted 2026-10-19.
- **ClearABN**: free ABN validator and GST-history check; bulk supplier checks are paid after 25 suppliers.
  Its post says it is mostly run by an AI agent. It claims Xero's ABN-verification idea dates from 2018 and is
  marked "Not in pipeline". **Check that on Xero Product Ideas in 2.2** before relying on it.
