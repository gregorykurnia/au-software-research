## First read (for 2.3, not a conclusion)

- **Price rises and narrow use** (Xero): buyers who use only payroll, or only bank feeds and payroll, pay for
  the full plan (PR-X10, X23, X29, X46, X47, X48). Compared to what? Xero's own cheaper plans, MYOB, Reckon,
  QuickBooks, payroll-only apps (MYOB Payroller is one; see PR-M25).
- **Xero API limits** (PR-X13–X15): the daily limit cut to 1,000 requests, with A$35/month to lift it. This
  matters for Gate 4 ("the platform allows the integration and API costs fit the price").
- **Records after you leave** (PR-M21, PR-M40, plus MYOB Idea Exchange MI-62..64 and the declined offline-backup
  ideas): people pay just to keep access to old books.
- **Tradie lead spend** (hipages): A$80–141/month subscriptions and A$100–200 per lead, with poor return. That is
  B1 that tradies spend on getting work. Whether it is a software problem is unknown.
- **The status quo wins often:** PR-X41, plus Whirlpool's spreadsheet comments.
